# Management Strategies for Enhancing Flood Resilience in Bihar

## Overview

This project presents a desk-based research study on flood risk and disaster management in Bihar.

The study examines Bihar's flood vulnerability, major river systems, historical flood events, socio-economic impacts, institutional mechanisms, preparedness, response, relief distribution, technology applications, and strategies for improving long-term flood resilience.

## Objectives

1. Evaluate Bihar's flood disaster management framework.
2. Assess flood preparedness, response, and relief mechanisms.
3. Identify key challenges and recommend strategies for enhancing flood resilience.

## Methodology

The study follows a descriptive qualitative research design based on secondary data.

The analysis uses:

* Government reports
* Policy documents
* Institutional publications
* Research papers
* Disaster management reports
* Secondary datasets
* Literature review and document analysis

The collected information was analysed thematically according to the study objectives and research chapters.

* The study is based primarily on secondary data.
* No primary field survey was conducted.
* Findings are specific to Bihar.
* Data availability and administrative information may vary across sources and periods.
